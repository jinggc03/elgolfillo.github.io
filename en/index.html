6 steps · conceptual walkthrough
01

Start with the problem.

The objective and constraints of a process guide design decisions. Before choosing a model, define what the system needs to do.

02

Give AI the context it needs.

Sources, permissions and data quality shape the result. Retrieval with RAG adds information; that information also needs validation.

03

Coordinate within clear boundaries.

Agents connect models, context and tools. Bounded tasks, permissions and action review are part of their design.

04

Connect to the real world.

APIs and protocols such as MCP provide access to tools. Contracts, permissions and errors are part of that integration.

05

Know when to step in.

Autonomy needs boundaries. Higher-impact decisions call for escalation criteria and mechanisms to review what the system does.

06

Understand what happened.

Traces, errors and evaluations help examine a system’s behaviour. They provide a basis for identifying problems and improving how it works.
